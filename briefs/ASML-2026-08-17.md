━━ Bull / Bear ━━
🐂 Bull:
1. AI/HBM-driven capex supercycle is structurally larger than the prior cycle — 2026 guidance raised twice already (€36-40B → €43-45B), with memory revenue guided +75% for the year; if this compounds through 2027-2028, EPS could scale well ahead of the 30-100% upside band in 3-5 years.
2. High-NA EUV is a second monopoly layer stacking on top of the first — early adopters (Intel, Samsung, SK Hynix) are already in high-volume qualification; as more nodes require High-NA, ASML captures ~2x the ASP per tool (€380M vs €180M), expanding margins further.
3. Wide-moat service/installed-base revenue (+28.1% YoY H1 2026) is high-margin, recurring, and largely insulated from new-tool order cyclicality — it derisks the growth story and supports the FCF engine even if new system orders wobble.

🐻 Bear:
1. Thesis breaks if AI/foundry capex hits an air-pocket in 2027-2028 and revenue growth falls back below 15% YoY (unchanged trigger from the 2026-07-19 brief) — TSMC's decision to skip High-NA through 2029 is an early signal that not all customers are willing to pay up, which could slow the next capex leg.
2. Escalating US/China export restrictions (the proposed MATCH Act would extend curbs to DUV tools, not just EUV) threaten a China revenue base already compressing from 33% of 2025 sales toward a guided ~20% in 2026 — a further legislative tightening could accelerate that decline and hit service revenue on China's large installed base.
3. Valuation is genuinely ambiguous right now (GuruFocus flags ~57% overvaluation vs. price, Morningstar implies ~10% undervaluation) — paying up at a $1,876 entry with no valuation consensus adds real drawdown risk if sentiment on AI capex durability shifts.
