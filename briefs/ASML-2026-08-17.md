🐻 Bear:
1. Conquest's DCF makes explicit what the other two obscure: at $1,883, ASML trades near ~54x TTM FCF, which requires growth well above what a normal (even monopoly-adjusted) semiconductor capex cycle has historically sustained for 5+ years. If growth decelerates back toward conquest's fade schedule (27%→6%) rather than staying near 30%+, the stock has substantial re-rating risk even with the business executing well.
2. Escalating US/China export restrictions (proposed MATCH Act would extend curbs to DUV tools) threaten a China revenue base already compressing from 33% of 2025 sales toward a guided ~20% in 2026.
3. TSMC's decision to skip High-NA through 2029 signals not all customers are willing to pay the ~2x ASP premium yet — if High-NA adoption lags Intel's early example, the "second monopoly layer" bull case that justifies Morningstar's higher FV takes longer to materialize than priced in.
